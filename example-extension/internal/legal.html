License and branding guidelines for Axis

Axis is open source and free to use under the MIT License.


You are welcome to fork, modify, and redistribute Axis. However, if you distribute a modified version, you must replace all Axis branding — including the name, logo, icons, and other visual identity — with your own. This helps distinguish community forks from the official Axis project.
